📐 Mathematics (SAT 1 & EST 1)

Complete mathematics curriculum with theory, examples, and practice problems.

1. Algebra & Polynomials

Topics covered:

  • Quadratic equations and roots
  • Polynomial operations and factorization
  • Systems of equations
  • Inequalities and absolute values
Quadratic Formula: x = [-b ± √(b² - 4ac)] / 2a
Example: Solve x² - 5x + 6 = 0
Using factorization: (x - 2)(x - 3) = 0
Solutions: x = 2 or x = 3

2. Trigonometry

Topics covered:

  • Trigonometric ratios (sin, cos, tan)
  • Unit circle and special angles
  • Trigonometric identities
  • Graphs of trigonometric functions
sin²θ + cos²θ = 1
tan θ = sin θ / cos θ
sin(A ± B) = sin A cos B ± cos A sin B

3. Logarithms & Exponentials

Topics covered:

  • Laws of exponents
  • Logarithmic equations
  • Change of base formula
  • Exponential growth and decay
log_a(x) = y ⟺ a^y = x
log_a(mn) = log_a(m) + log_a(n)
log_a(m/n) = log_a(m) - log_a(n)

4. Calculus Basics

Topics covered:

  • Limits and continuity
  • Derivatives and differentiation rules
  • Applications of derivatives
  • Basic integration
Power Rule: d/dx(x^n) = nx^(n-1)
Product Rule: d/dx(uv) = u'v + uv'
Chain Rule: d/dx(f(g(x))) = f'(g(x))·g'(x)

5. Geometry & Vectors

Topics covered:

  • Conic sections (circles, ellipses, parabolas)
  • Vector operations and properties
  • Dot and cross products
  • 3D geometry

⚛️ Physics (SAT 1 & EST 1)

Complete physics curriculum covering mechanics, energy, electricity, magnetism, and waves.

1. Mechanics & Motion

Topics covered:

  • Kinematics (velocity, acceleration, equations of motion)
  • Newton's Laws of Motion
  • Friction and resistance
  • Circular motion and gravitation
v = u + at
s = ut + ½at²
F = ma (Newton's Second Law)
g = GM/r²
Example: A car accelerates at 5 m/s² for 10 seconds. Find final velocity.
Given: u = 0, a = 5 m/s², t = 10 s
v = u + at = 0 + 5(10) = 50 m/s

2. Energy & Work

Topics covered:

  • Kinetic and potential energy
  • Work-energy theorem
  • Conservation of energy
  • Power and efficiency
KE = ½mv²
PE = mgh
Work = Force × Distance = Fd
Power = Work / Time = E / t

3. Waves & Oscillations

Topics covered:

  • Simple harmonic motion
  • Wave properties (wavelength, frequency, amplitude)
  • Sound waves and Doppler effect
  • Light waves and electromagnetic spectrum
v = fλ (Wave equation)
f = 1/T (Frequency and period)
T = 2π√(m/k) (SHM period)
f' = f(v ± v_observer)/(v ∓ v_source) (Doppler effect)

4. Electricity & Current

Topics covered:

  • Electric charge and Coulomb's Law
  • Electric fields and potential
  • Current, voltage, and resistance
  • Circuits and Kirchhoff's Laws
F = kq₁q₂/r² (Coulomb's Law)
V = IR (Ohm's Law)
P = VI = I²R (Power)
EMF = ε = IR + Ir (Circuit equation)

5. Magnetism & Electromagnetism

Topics covered:

  • Magnetic fields and forces
  • Electromagnetic induction
  • Faraday's Law
  • Transformers and AC circuits
F = BIL sin θ (Force on current-carrying conductor)
ε = -N(dΦ/dt) (Faraday's Law)
Φ = BA cos θ (Magnetic flux)

6. Modern Physics

Topics covered:

  • Quantum theory and photons
  • Atomic structure and spectra
  • Nuclear physics and radioactivity
  • Relativity basics
E = hf (Photon energy)
E = mc² (Mass-energy equivalence)
λ = h/p (de Broglie wavelength)
A(t) = A₀e^(-λt) (Radioactive decay)

📚 Study Resources & Tips

📋 Study Tips

  • ✅ Practice problems regularly to strengthen concepts
  • ✅ Review formulas and key concepts before exams
  • ✅ Solve past papers to familiarize yourself with exam patterns
  • ✅ Create summary notes for quick revision
  • ✅ Time yourself while solving practice problems
  • ✅ Focus on weak areas and practice them more
  • ✅ Use diagrams and visual aids for better understanding

⏱️ Exam Preparation Timeline

  • 8-12 weeks before: Start with fundamentals and weak areas
  • 4-8 weeks before: Complete all topics and solve practice problems
  • 2-4 weeks before: Solve full past papers under exam conditions
  • 1-2 weeks before: Revise and practice weak topics
  • Last week: Light revision and rest

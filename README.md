# SAT 1 & EST 1 Study Materials

## 📚 Overview

A comprehensive, free educational platform for studying **SAT 1 & EST 1** examinations in **Mathematics and Physics**.

**Teacher:** Ahmed Labib

## 📋 Contents

### Mathematics (SAT 1 & EST 1)
- **Algebra & Polynomials** - Equations, factorization, systems
- **Trigonometry** - Ratios, identities, functions
- **Logarithms & Exponentials** - Properties and applications
- **Calculus Basics** - Limits, derivatives, integration
- **Geometry & Vectors** - Shapes, coordinates, vector operations

### Physics (SAT 1 & EST 1)
- **Mechanics & Motion** - Kinematics, forces, gravitation
- **Energy & Work** - Kinetic/potential energy, conservation
- **Waves & Oscillations** - SHM, sound, light, Doppler effect
- **Electricity & Current** - Charges, fields, circuits
- **Magnetism & Electromagnetism** - Fields, induction, transformers
- **Modern Physics** - Quantum theory, atomic structure, nuclear physics
